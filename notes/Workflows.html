Agentic Workflow ​

有Agent能够自主决策的流程, 核心为AI agent参与流程，能规划，调用工具（Tool Calling），执行任务。自主决策自主执行，可以是一个固定的流程，e.g.一个agent自动读取邮件，总结并回复。适合场景：单一任务自动化。

A vertical slice of an application that corresponds to a customer use case. In GenAI, vertical slices like this often correspond to a user-driven(manual) workflow that is in the process of becoming AI assisted or fully automated.

Composable Agentic Workflows ​

可以被拆成模块，再像积木一样组合/复用的agentic workflow。核心为多个agent/tool/step 被设计成可插拔的模块，可以组合成不同的流程。特点为模块化、复用、编排、扩展。通常是多个reusable components。e.g."读取邮件 agent" + "分类 agent" + "写回复 agent" + "审批 step"可以组合成不同的业务流程。适用场景：企业级复杂流程、多团队、多系统集成。

System Architecture ​

The system architecture involves five interacting components:

  • Agents that implement each step of the workflow
  • A multiagent architecture that advances end users through the workflow
  • Gvernance, monitoring, and security components
  • A learning pipeline to continuously improve AI capability
  • An ongoing data creation, collection, and curation program

*** An influential article from Anthropic on building effective agents(https://oreil.ly/7oEug) notes that "the most successful implementations use simple, composable patterns rather than complex frameworks" ***
